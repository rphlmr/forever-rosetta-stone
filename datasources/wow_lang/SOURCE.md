# WoWLang — Polish, Turkish and Ukrainian context

Credits: Platine (lead developer/author), Dragonarab, Hakan YILMAZ, and translators,
testers and contributors of WoWpoPolsku, WoWTR and WoWAR as credited upstream.

Preserve the complete upstream LICENSE and README. The inspected LICENSE contains GPL-3.0,
while the README describes MIT. This conflict is not resolved or silently relabelled:
records carry `licenseDeclarationConflict:contextOnly` and are excluded from automatic
catalog fills. Underlying game text remains Blizzard's content.

Only our supported Polish, Turkish and Ukrainian quest tables are selected. Other project
languages are not added to our supported locales. These are cross-version context sources;
retain source placeholders, authorship declarations, revision and meaning. Unspecified AI
usage is not evidence of human-only translation.
