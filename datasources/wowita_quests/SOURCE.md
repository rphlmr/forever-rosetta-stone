# WoWita-quests — Italian Classic context

Upstream: https://github.com/deemonder/WoWita-quests (renamed from Girolamone/WoWita-quests).
Credits: Icedany for the Italian translation/development; WoWita maintainers and the original
wowpopolsku.pl project. Preserve the upstream README with each pinned snapshot.

The inspected repository supplies no translation-data licence. No MIT or other grant is
inferred. Records are context-only and carry `licenseNotSpecified`; they must not be
copied automatically into shipped catalogs. Source and derivative rights remain with their
owners; underlying game text belongs to Blizzard Entertainment.

The dataset targets Classic Anniversary. Legacy player tokens and field meaning are
preserved for review; matching a quest ID does not establish Forever compatibility.
AI authorship is unspecified, not confirmed human authorship.
